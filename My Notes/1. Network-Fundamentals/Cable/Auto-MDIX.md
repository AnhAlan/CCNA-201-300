Automatic Medium-Dependent Interface Crossover is a feature on modern switches and routers that automatically detects the cable type (straight or crossover) and switches TX/RX connections if necessary.

1. Before auto-MIDIX
    Connect PC ↔ Switch → use a straight cable
    Connect PC ↔ PC / Switch ↔ Switch → use a crossover cable

2. with auto-MIDIX

    The switch will automatically determine the TX/RX on the port.
    Any cable (straight or crossover) can be used, and the connection will still work.

    Benefits:
    No need to worry about choosing the right cable type when deploying the network.
    Reduces connection errors due to incorrect cable routing.

☢️ Note: Auto-MDIX is common on modern switches; most 1000Base-T switches and above support this feature.
